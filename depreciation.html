Real tax bills depend on AMT, the 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT), passive-activity loss carryforwards, your full bracket, and state-specific rules (some states tax LTCG differently from ordinary income, and a few have no income tax at all). The IRS mid-month convention also slightly reduces depreciation in the first and last years held. Consult a CPA before filing or making a sale decision. §1031 exchanges have strict timing (45-day identification, 180-day close) and structural requirements.
